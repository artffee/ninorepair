Florida scope of work

We'll tell you what
we can't do

Florida draws a hard line between handyman work and licensed contracting. Plenty of guys blur it. We don't — you get the truth up front, and the right licensed pro when you need one.

✓

We handle it

Non-structural, no permit, no license required.

  • Drywall, paint, texture, trim & carpentry
  • Tile, grout, caulk & sealing
  • Doors, locks, smart locks & hardware
  • Mounting, shelving & assembly
  • Screens, lanai & pool cage panels
  • Pressure washing & exterior care
  • Hurricane shutters & storm prep
  • Fences, gates, decks & dock boards
  • Grab bars & aging-in-place safety
  • Wood rot repair on fascia & trim
⇄

We coordinate it

Licensed partner does the trade work. We manage the job and do everything around it.

  • Electrical — outlets, fans, fixtures, panels
  • Plumbing — water heaters, toilets, faucets, lines
  • HVAC — mini-splits, air handlers, ducts
  • Roofing & anything roof-mounted
  • Impact windows & permanent shutters
  • Any job that requires a building permit

You still get one point of contact — and we patch, texture and paint everything back when they're done.

✕

Nobody should

We'll refer you and walk away from the job.

  • Structural or load-bearing changes
  • Gas lines & gas appliances
  • Mold remediation & asbestos abatement
  • Septic, wells & pool equipment

Pre-1979 popcorn ceiling? It gets tested for asbestos before anyone scrapes it. Then we can do the repair.

Straight talk: under Florida Statutes Ch. 489, minor repairs that don't require a permit and aren't part of a larger contracted project don't require a state contractor license. Electrical, plumbing, HVAC, roofing and structural work do. NinoRepair works inside the handyman scope and brings in Florida-licensed partners for the rest. Nothing here is legal advice.
