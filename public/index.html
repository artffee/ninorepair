Florida scope

Clear about what we do
and what needs a pro

NinoRepair stays inside handyman scope and coordinates licensed trades when the work requires them.

✓

We handle it

  • Drywall, paint, trim and finish repairs
  • Mounting, shelves and assembly
  • Doors, locks and hardware
  • Screens and exterior care
  • Pressure washing and storm prep
⇄

We coordinate it

  • Electrical work requiring a licensed electrician
  • Plumbing work requiring a licensed plumber
  • HVAC, roofing and permitted work
  • Other licensed-trade work outside handyman scope
✕

We do not guess

  • Structural changes
  • Gas work
  • Mold/asbestos remediation
  • Anything unsafe or outside lawful scope

Straight talk: job requirements vary by scope and jurisdiction. Licensed electrical, plumbing, HVAC, roofing, structural and permitted work should be performed by appropriately licensed professionals. NinoRepair handles handyman-scope work and can coordinate qualified trades where appropriate.
