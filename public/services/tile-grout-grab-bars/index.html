Questions people ask

Can you install grab bars on a tile wall?

Yes. I drill the tile cleanly and anchor into studs, blocking or masonry behind it.

Should I regrout or re-caulk?

Caulk goes in corners and changes of plane; grout goes in the joints between tiles. Most tired showers need both.

Why does my caulk keep turning black?

Mold under or behind the old bead. It has to be fully removed and the joint dried before new mold-resistant silicone goes in.

Can't I just caulk over the old caulk?

You can. It'll look great for about three weeks, then the mold underneath comes back through. The old bead has to come out.
