Questions people ask

How do minimum charges and materials work?

Starting prices apply to the specific job described; per-panel or per-opening rates are subject to any stated visit minimum. Hourly work is $95/hour with a one-hour minimum. The $65 trip/diagnostic fee is waived when work is performed on the same visit. Materials are billed at cost + 15% unless you supply them. Barrier-island visits and gated high-rises requiring COI or elevator scheduling add a $45 building-access surcharge. Nino confirms the scope and final price before work begins.

How much does a drywall patch cost in Fort Lauderdale?

Small patches under 6 inches start at $130, including texture match and touch-up paint. Larger repairs, ceilings and multiple holes are quoted from photos — the AI estimator gives you a ballpark range in about twenty seconds.

Can you match the texture on my walls?

Yes. Most South Florida homes and condos use knockdown or orange peel. I match the pattern on a scrap board first when the texture is unusual.

Will I see where the patch was?

Not if the wall gets painted corner to corner. Touching up just the patch can flash in certain light, especially with older paint — I'll tell you up front which one your wall needs.

Do you remove popcorn ceilings?

Yes. If the home was built before 1979, the ceiling gets tested for asbestos first. If it comes back positive, abatement goes to a licensed contractor and I finish the ceiling afterward.

Can you make it look like the hole never happened?

That's the whole job. Your landlord, your spouse and your security deposit will never know.
