Questions people ask

How do minimum charges and materials work?

Starting prices apply to the specific job described; per-panel or per-opening rates are subject to any stated visit minimum. Hourly work is $95/hour with a one-hour minimum. The $65 trip/diagnostic fee is waived when work is performed on the same visit. Materials are billed at cost + 15% unless you supply them. Barrier-island visits and gated high-rises requiring COI or elevator scheduling add a $45 building-access surcharge. Nino confirms the scope and final price before work begins.

How much do door and lock repairs cost?

Lockset or deadbolt swaps start at $85. Door adjustments start at $95, smart lock installation and setup at $130, and sliding glass door roller replacement at $265 per door. Parts and scope affect the final price; send a photo for a ballpark.

Why is my sliding glass door so hard to open?

Usually worn or seized rollers, a dirty track, or a panel that's dropped out of level. Rollers are a common, fixable repair — photo of the bottom of the door helps me bring the right part.

Can you install and set up a smart lock?

Yes. I install it, pair it to your phone and set up codes. For hosts, I can rotate codes between stays.

Do you replace sliding glass doors?

Full door replacement usually needs a permit, especially impact doors. I bring in a licensed partner for that and handle everything around it.

My slider sounds like a shopping cart. Is that normal?

No. That's worn rollers grinding on the track. New rollers and a clean track and it'll roll with one finger again.
