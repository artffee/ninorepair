FLORIDA SCOPE

Screen and spline work is handyman scope. Structural repair to the aluminum frame — bent beams, loose uprights, storm damage to the cage itself — is a licensed contractor job. I'll tell you which one you're looking at from a photo.

Questions people ask

How much does it cost to rescreen a pool cage panel?

Single panels run $45–$85 each, with a $185 minimum visit. Full-enclosure rescreens are priced as one job — a photo of the whole cage gets you a ballpark.

How long does rescreening last in South Florida?

Sun and salt air are hard on screen. Spline tends to shrink and crack before the screen itself fails, which is why I replace both.

Can you fix a bent pool cage beam?

Structural frame repair needs a licensed contractor in Florida. I refer that out and rescreen once the frame is sound.

Do you install pet doors in screens?

Yes, in lower panels on pool cages, lanais and screen doors.

Can you get the lizard out too?

I'll shoo him politely. After that, the new screen is his problem, not yours.
