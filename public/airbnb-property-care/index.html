Host questions

What does an Airbnb handyman retainer include?

Essential ($299/mo) covers two scheduled visits a month, a priority response window and a photo punch list after visits. Pro ($499/mo) doubles it to four visits, adds same-day priority response and a quarterly deep punch list, and restock runs are available.

Do I need a retainer to book you?

No. One-off visits are welcome. Text a photo of the problem and I'll give you a ballpark before I come out.

Can you document damage for an AirCover claim?

Yes. I produce timestamped photo packages of the damage and the repair, ready to attach to an AirCover or host insurance claim.

I don't live in Florida. Can you be my eyes on the unit?

That's most of my host clients. Every visit ends with a photo report, and hurricane prep includes shutters up, furniture in and a post-storm check.

Can you tell guests the Wi-Fi password for me?

I'd rather fix what's broken than answer your inbox. But the keypad I install means nobody's calling you about a key again.
